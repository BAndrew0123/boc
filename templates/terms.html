Legal & Giving Guidelines

Terms of Service

Terms governing donations, website usage, and volunteer engagement.

1. Charitable Giving & Allocations

All financial contributions made to Body of Christ Centre (via M-Pesa Paybill 400200, STK Push, Card, or Direct Bank Wire) are voluntary charitable gifts dedicated to supporting 160 resident children in Limuru, Kenya.

2. Official Receipts

Donors are entitled to receive an official digital receipt with a unique transaction reference for every contribution made through our website.

3. Code of Conduct for Volunteers & Visitors

Volunteers participating in community outreach, medical camps, or campus mentorship agree to respect child protection policies and adhere to the guidelines set by Body of Christ Centre management.
