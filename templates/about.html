About Body of Christ Centre

Discovering our 20+ year journey of faith, compassion, and empowering orphaned & vulnerable children in Limuru, Kenya.

Our Founder & History

Body of Christ Center was founded in 2004 by Pastor Mary Watare Mbugua. Driven by a divine vision and a deeply compassionate heart following a tragic personal loss, Pastor Mary dedicated her life to opening a sanctuary for orphans, street children, and vulnerable youths in Limuru.

What began as a modest shelter with a handful of children has grown into a vibrant residential boarding center caring for 160 children. We provide comprehensive nutrition, spiritual guidance, health screenings, and complete primary, secondary, and tertiary educational sponsorships.

Our Mission

To provide holistic care, quality education, psychological support, and a nurturing family environment that empowers vulnerable children to reach their highest potential.

Our Vision

A world where every orphaned and vulnerable child experiences God's love, receives quality education, and thrives as a productive, dignified leader in society.

Core Values

Unconditional Love, Integrity & Stewardship, Academic Excellence, Spiritual Growth, and Community Advocacy.

Our Location

Mutarakwa Village, Limuru

Nestled in the serene highlands of Limuru, Kiambu County, our facility includes children's dormitories, dining halls, study classrooms, a computer lab, an organic vegetable farm, and a community hall.
