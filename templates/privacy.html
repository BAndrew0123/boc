Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Body of Christ Centre collects, uses, and safeguards donor and volunteer information.

1. Information We Collect

When you donate, register as a volunteer, or submit an inquiry through Body of Christ Centre website, we collect personal information necessary to process your request, including:

  • Full Name and Contact Information (Phone Number, Email Address)
  • Transaction Details (M-Pesa Reference Codes, Payment Amounts, Target Cause)
  • Volunteer Interests and Inquiries

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used for charitable and administrative purposes:

  • Processing donations and issuing official digital receipts
  • Verifying M-Pesa or bank transactions
  • Coordinating volunteer outreach events
  • Complying with Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 legal standards

3. Data Security & Non-Disclosure

We enforce strict security controls to protect donor data. Body of Christ Centre never sells, rents, trades, or shares your personal details with third-party advertisers or commercial entities.

4. Contact Us

For any questions regarding your data privacy, email us at info@bodyofchristcentre.co.ke or call +254 720 403 647.
