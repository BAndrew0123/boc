Our Journey of Hope

A Safe Haven Born Out of Compassion & Faith

Welcome to Body of Christ Center. Our story began in 2004 when Pastor Mary Watare Mbugua, driven by a profound vision and fueled by a compassionate heart following a personal tragedy, established the center to shelter orphans and vulnerable children.

Over the past decade, we have expanded beyond basic shelter to nurture our children through primary, secondary high schools, and universities—producing future lawyers, journalists, doctors, and community leaders.
